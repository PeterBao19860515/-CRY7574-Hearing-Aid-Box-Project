This is in-box measurement run 6 of 6. The Excel measurements are presented at 90, 80, 70, 60, 50 and 40 dB SPL. Every series is limited to 100 Hz–10 kHz and displayed with 1/6 Oct. smoothing. The logarithmic frequency axis retains the source sample frequencies and numerical precision within that range.
